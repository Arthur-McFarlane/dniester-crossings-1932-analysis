# Data

`persons.csv`, `incidents.csv`, `dubasari_daily_temperature_1928_1933.csv` and `CODEBOOK.md` are copies of version 1.0 of the dataset, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23299300 (CC BY 4.0). See the Zenodo record for sources, method and limitations.

`gazetteer.csv` gives coordinates for every place named in `location_modern` and `origin_modern`, with the source of each (GeoNames, Wikipedia or Natural Earth), its precision, a note on the identification, and which bank of the Dniester it lies on. Two places of origin (Ianishevka, Kiev oblast; Mar'ianovka, Grossulovo raion) could not be identified; regions such as "Bessarabia" have no point.

`naturalearth/` holds Natural Earth 1:10m land, lakes and the Dniester, clipped to 25–63°E, 42–59°N (public domain; https://www.naturalearthdata.com).
